Program Overview

We empower young tennis players with neuroscience-backed emotional intelligence skills to enhance their personal strengths, address growth areas, and significantly improve their on-court performance and overall development.

Our background:

  • Happy Family: Emotional intelligence coaching for parents and kids to strengthen relationships and self-awareness.
  • Happy Team: Corporate training for leaders and teams in companies like BHP and SQM to improve communication, reduce stress, and enhance performance.
  • Happy Sports: Tailored programs for athletes, combining mental resilience, emotional control, and performance mindset—already applied successfully with tennis, padel, and other sports.

Step-by-Step Program Process

1. Initial Assessment & Interviews (1.5 hours)

  • Individual interviews with player, parents, and coaches
  • Character Strengths Test to identify emotional strengths and growth opportunities

2. Personalized Program Design (1 hour)

Creation of a tailored emotional skills plan based on interviews, test results, and the athlete’s tennis profile.

3. Implementation (16 sessions)

  • 1 hour preparation per session with customized exercises and emotional strategies
  • 1 hour execution per session:
    • 50% on-court – emotional control in real tennis scenarios
    • 25% gym-based – stress management, resilience, and mindfulness drills
    • 25% online/office – video analysis, discussion, and theory reinforcement

Additional support includes an e-learning platform for parents and players, plus tournament attendance for observation and feedback.

4. Final Action Plan & Recommendations (1 hour)

A co-created action plan to ensure the learned skills are applied long-term beyond the program.

Neuroscience-Backed Methodology Includes

  • Emotional Self-Awareness
  • Emotional Regulation & Stress Management
  • Focused Attention & Sports Mindfulness
  • Emotional Resilience
  • Effective Communication & Empathy
  • Growth Mindset Development
  • Strategic Goal-Setting Skills

Customization & Flexibility

Every plan is personalized per athlete and modified as needed. Situations are created based on each player’s strengths and improvement opportunities.

Pilot Program (5 Players)

Start with 5 carefully chosen players to demonstrate results and refine the program. Detailed reports and clear evidence of improvement for academy and parents.

Success Case: Javiera Carvajal

Javiera came to us as a talented young tennis player struggling with frustration on court and frequent emotional “meltdowns” after losses. Through the Happy Sports Emotional Mastery Program, we worked on her self-awareness, resilience, and emotional regulation using tailored on-court scenarios and mindfulness practices.

After 8 sessions and continuous feedback with her parents and coach, she learned to:

  • Recognize her emotional triggers and reset mid-game.
  • Turn frustration into focus, improving consistency and composure.
  • Communicate openly with coach and family, reducing tension off-court.

Six months later, Javiera reached her first regional tournament final and reported greater enjoyment of tennis, proving emotional control directly improves performance and passion for the sport.
